The problem · teachers

To go online, a teacher leaks their lessons — or pays a fortune.

  • Sell over WhatsApp or YouTube, and one buyer re-shares to the whole class — paid once.
  • Or pay an agency thousands to build a platform — then rely on them for every change.
  • No way to cut off access when the term ends.
  • Real income and control lost, every single term.

The problem · the classroom

And the exam still eats their week.

  • Online, nothing stops a student from cheating through the whole test.
  • Offline, the teacher marks every paper by hand — hours gone, every week.
  • No quick way to run a pop quiz in the in-person session and see who's struggling.
  • Teaching is one job; grading and policing are three more — all unpaid.

Why now

A market that already pays — just not to a platform.

  • Private tutoring is one of Egypt's largest household education spends.
  • Teachers — and whole learning centers — already run it monthly, in cash, over WhatsApp.
  • No tool fits that workflow: per-term or monthly, mobile-first, in Arabic.
  • We're not creating the behavior — we're giving it rails.

How it works

Three steps. One access rule.

STEP 01

Upload

The lesson is transcoded and encrypted automatically.

STEP 02

Redeem

The student enters a code and is enrolled for the period.

STEP 03

Watch

Every play is checked — enrolled, in-window, device limit — and watermarked.

The same check runs on web and in the app. Access follows the student, not the URL.

How we make money

Priced the way private lessons already are.

  • Monthly or per-term — the teacher chooses.
  • Lessons unlock by month, so each payment buys that month's material.
  • Code-based collection to start — no payment-gateway fees while we prove demand.
  • We take a share of every sale.

Why it holds

Two things most video startups get wrong.

Economics Cost

Video runs on Cloudflare R2: free egress, storage around $0.015/GB. One small server serves the whole pilot. Video traffic — what bankrupts video startups — doesn't scale our costs.

Protection Moat

Encrypted lessons, keys gated to enrolled devices, access that expires with the term, a watermark on every stream. Traceable deterrence — without enterprise-DRM cost.
